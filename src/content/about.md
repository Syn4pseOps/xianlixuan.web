## The Alias

In the digital world, whether through CTF competitions, security research, or personal projects, I go by the name **Syn4pseOps**.

The name reflects the way I approach learning. **Syn4pse** is inspired by the concept of a synapse — a point of connection where information is transmitted and ideas come together. To me, it represents bringing knowledge from different domains together: **Security, Binary, Systems, and AI**.

The **4** represents the four-step process I follow when learning:

> Observe → Understand → Break → Build

And **Ops** represents action. I believe true understanding does not come from reading alone, but from experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and building them again.

> «Connect knowledge. Turn understanding into action.»
